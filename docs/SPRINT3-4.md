# Research and editorial delivery — 12 September 2026

This delivery adds suggested headlines to the existing ten imported council stories, preserving the original headline and source. Each has an alternative, reader value and reporting questions. Suggestions are editorial drafts prepared for this batch; future imports do not automatically generate suggestions. Editors can write and revise ideas in the source drawer.

Research desk: manually group reports, link original stories, record claims and attach supporting, contradictory or contextual excerpts with source links. Publisher counts prompt second-source research and do not certify independence or truth.

Editorial desk: create a draft from a research group, write headline/body, record risk assessment and fact/harm/rights checks, submit to review, request changes or approve. Owners/editors write; owners/reviewers decide. Owner self-review is allowed and audited. High-risk drafts cannot be approved in this slice. Reopening invalidates approval. Revision checks reject stale saves. All mutations retain database audit history. Nothing publishes externally.

Still outstanding: automatic grouping, source revision detection, scheduled ingestion, automated headline generation, specialist clearance workflow, finer moderation controls, and WordPress integration. These are working slices of Sprints 3 and 4, not completion of every blueprint item.

Validation: foundation, discovery, feed, research and editorial database tests; TypeScript; production build. Live database and deployment status must be recorded separately after rollout.
